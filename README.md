# 🛒 Amazon E-Commerce Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project features an interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze Amazon e-commerce data. It provides comprehensive insights into sales performance, customer demographics, product preferences, payment methods, and regional trends. The goal of this dashboard is to help stakeholders make data-driven decisions to optimize inventory, marketing, and sales strategies.

## 📊 Dashboard Preview

### Page 1: Executive Summary, Trends & Sales
*Focuses on high-level KPIs, monthly revenue trends, top product categories, payment distributions, and regional revenue.*


### Page 2: Device Preference, Delivery & Demographics
*Analyzes customer behavior across devices, delivery speed distribution, average ratings by age group, and state-level revenue mapping.*


### Page 3: Brand, Category & Product Summary
*Provides a deep dive into brand performance, top/lowest rated subcategories, and a detailed product brand summary table.*

## 🔍 Key Insights
*   **Total Orders:** 21K
*   **Total Revenue:** $5.97M
*   **Average Order Value:** $284.76
*   **Total Products Sold:** 43K
*   **Average Rating:** 3.73
*   **Leading Category:** Men's Clothing
*   **Best-Selling Brand:** Nike (leads in revenue, followed by Adidas and Carter's)
*   **Preferred Payment Method:** Gift Card (25.21%), closely followed by Credit Card and PayPal.
*   **Top Region:** Midwest ($1.88M in revenue)
*   **Trend Analysis:** Sales have shown steady growth over recent months, with peak performance observed during the December and November holiday season.

## 💡 Recommendations & Business Actions
Based on the data analyzed in this dashboard, the following actions are recommended:

1. **Capitalize on Holiday Peaks:** Sales spike significantly in November and December. Ensure inventory levels for top brands (Nike, Adidas) are increased by October to avoid stockouts.
2. **Leverage Gift Card Popularity:** Gift Cards are the #1 payment method. Launch a "Buy a $50 Gift Card, get $5 free" promotion in October to drive early holiday revenue.
3. **Target Mobile Users:** The 18-24 age group heavily prefers mobile devices (8K orders vs 6K desktop). Invest in mobile-specific ads and ensure the mobile checkout process is seamless.
4. **Regional Focus:** The Midwest is the top revenue driver ($1.88M). Run exclusive Midwest-focused loyalty programs to retain these customers, while testing new marketing strategies in the lower-performing South region.

## 🚀 How to Use This Repository
1. Download the `Amazon_Dashboard.pbix` file from this repository.
2. Open it using Power BI Desktop (ensure you have the latest version).
3. Interact with the slicers (Date, Category, Brand, Region, Age Group) to filter the data dynamically.
4. Hover over charts to see detailed tooltips.
